WHO WE ARE

Founded in 2010, ICI SERVICES has established itself as a major player in Côte d'Ivoire's industrial landscape. With more than a decade of expertise, we support companies in developing, optimising and securing their industrial facilities.

Our mission: to deliver reliable, innovative technical solutions tailored to the specific needs of Côte d'Ivoire's industrial sector, combining quality, responsiveness and local know-how while fostering local self-sufficiency.

At ICI SERVICES, we believe in sustainable, responsible industrial growth. That is why performance, safety and compliance with standards are at the heart of every project we undertake.
